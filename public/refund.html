Cancellation & Refund Policy

Value Education Contest 2027 · Organised by International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), Hyderabad

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Registration fee

The registration fee covers the contest booklet, study material and calendar. It is paid online through Razorpay when you register.

Cancellations

Once the registration fee has been paid, a registration cannot be cancelled and the fee is not refunded.

When we refund

  • Charged more than once for the same registration — we refund the extra payment(s).
  • Charged but the registration was not recorded — we refund the payment.

To request one of these refunds, contact us with the student's name, school, the mobile number used and the payment reference. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay; banks usually take 5–7 working days to show the amount.

Failed payments

If a payment fails but money was deducted from your account, it is returned automatically by Razorpay / your bank, usually within 5–7 working days. You do not need to contact us for this.

Delivery of materials

The booklet, study material and calendar are given out through the contest coordinator for your school. Nothing is shipped by post.
