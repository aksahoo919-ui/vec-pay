Privacy Policy

Value Education Contest 2027 · ISKCON ABIDS, Hyderabad

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Value Education Contest 2027 registration website collects, why, and how it is handled. The contest is organised by ISKCON ABIDS, Hyderabad ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Whether you are studying in a school or college, working, or other.
  • Your school and class, or your college and branch, or your company name.
  • Your preferred language for the contest.
  • If you register as a parent: your child's name, school and class/section.
  • Payment status and the payment reference number from Razorpay.

We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

Why we use it

  • To register you for the contest and confirm your payment.
  • To arrange contest booklets through your school coordinator or the temple.
  • To organise the contest and contact you about it.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The contest organisers and the coordinators for your school or college. Coordinators receive lists of registered participants through Google Sheets links.
  • Razorpay, to process your payment, under Razorpay's privacy policy.
  • Our service providers, which store the data for us: Cloudflare (website and database) and Google (Google Sheets).

Children

Many participants are school students. If you are under 18, please register with the knowledge and consent of your parent or guardian. Parents may also register on behalf of their children.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details only as long as needed to run the contest and distribute booklets and results, and payment records for as long as required for accounting.

Cookies

The public registration page does not use tracking cookies. A login cookie is used only for contest administrators. Razorpay's payment window may set its own cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you gave us by contacting the organisers at ISKCON ABIDS temple, Hyderabad, or through the coordinator who referred you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page with a new date.
