Privacy Policy

Value Education Contest 2027 · Organised by International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), Hyderabad

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Value Education Contest 2027 registration website collects, why, and how it is handled. The contest is organised by International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), Hyderabad ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • The participant's name.
  • City/area, school, class and (optionally) section.
  • The preferred language for the contest.
  • A mobile number to contact the participant or their parent.
  • Payment status and the payment reference number from Razorpay.

We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

Why we use it

  • To register you for the contest and confirm your payment.
  • To arrange contest booklets through your school coordinator.
  • To add you to the right WhatsApp group for contest updates.
  • To organise the contest and contact you about it.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The contest organisers and the coordinators for your school. Coordinators receive lists of registered participants through Google Sheets links.
  • Members of the WhatsApp group you choose to join can see your WhatsApp name and number, as in any WhatsApp group.
  • Razorpay, to process your payment, under Razorpay's privacy policy.
  • Our service providers, which store the data for us: Cloudflare (website and database) and Google (Google Sheets).

Children

Participants are school students. Please register with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian, or have them register for you.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details only as long as needed to run the contest and distribute booklets and results, and payment records for as long as required for accounting.

Cookies

The public registration page does not use tracking cookies. A login cookie is used only for contest administrators. Razorpay's payment window may set its own cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you gave us by contacting us or through your school's contest coordinator.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page with a new date.
